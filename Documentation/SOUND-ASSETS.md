## Distribution and integrity

Only the embedded MP3 payloads are required at runtime. Loose duplicate MP3 files are intentionally omitted from the source archive. Original WAV/FLAC source recordings are not redistributed. `SOUND-ASSET-MANIFEST.json` records the source project, declared license, original-source checksums where available, and SHA-256 checksums for every embedded MP3 payload.
